## Description (4000)

Truyện is a shelf of Vietnamese folk tales and legends to listen to and watch scene by scene, like turning the pages
of a picture book. The stories are told in Vietnamese, with Vietnamese subtitles; the app itself speaks Vietnamese and
English.

TWO SHELVES
• Children's shelf: Mèo Miu the cat tells gentle retellings for young children: Sơn Tinh and Thủy Tinh, Saint Gióng,
  Tấm and Cám, The Star Fruit Tree, Children of the Dragon and the Fairy.
• Adults' shelf: fuller tellings, close to the old texts: The Girl of Nam Xương, Chử Đồng Tử and Tiên Dung, The Legend
  of the Returned Sword.

LIKE A BOOK THAT READS ITSELF
• One page per scene: a painting, gentle motion, the storyteller's voice and music.
• Subtitles always on, in the size you pick.
• Pages curl as they turn; turn them yourself or let the story turn them.

LISTEN WITH THE PHONE LOCKED
• The story goes on with the screen locked or another app open.
• Lock screen and headphone controls: play, pause, previous and next scene.
• Sleep timer: stop after 10, 20 or 30 minutes, or at the end of the story.

OFFLINE
• Download stories to the phone, see the space they take, remove them any time.

SAFE FOR CHILDREN
• No account, no ads, no data collected, no tracking.
• Anything that leaves the app first asks a question for a grown-up.

Pictures, voices and music are made with AI and reviewed scene by scene before release; the words are retold from the
folk tales, with their sources credited in the app.
